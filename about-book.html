About The Book

Zephyr of Aloha by Perry Hotchkiss

On the rugged lava fields of Hawaii’s Big Island, Ezekiel “Zeke” has built a life defined by intention, discipline, and quiet strength. His solar-powered home, flourishing orchid farm, and exotic aviary stand as proof that paradise isn’t found it’s earned. After years of focused independence, Zeke believes his world is complete.

Until a chance roadside encounter changes everything.

When Adele and her young daughter, Daphne, move to Hawaii seeking a fresh start, an unexpected flat tire introduces them to a man whose kindness feels rare and steady. What begins as a simple act of help unfolds into shared meals, laughter beneath banyan trees, explorations of Hawaiʻi Volcanoes National Park, and the slow awakening of something deeper.

But love, like living on an active volcano, requires courage. It asks for vulnerability from those who have carefully constructed their lives to avoid uncertainty.

Set against breathtaking island landscapes lava flows, ocean cliffs, tropical farms, and vibrant macaws Zephyr of Aloha is a heartfelt story of resilience, second chances, and the quiet power of mature love. It reminds us that even on shifting ground, a life built with purpose can make room for connection.
